Privacy Policy

Arriverr PDF Editor (“we”, “us”, or “Arriverr”) operates the Arriverr PDF Editor mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the App.

Effective date: October 29, 2025

1. Summary

Arriverr PDF Editor is a comprehensive productivity tool for viewing, scanning, and editing PDF documents. The App includes features such as PDF Viewing, Scan to PDF, Image to PDF, Text to PDF, Password Protection, PDF Compression, Merging/Splitting, and OCR (Image to Text). The App displays advertisements via Google AdMob.

2. Data we collect

We distinguish between:

  • Data you provide directly: We do not require account creation or login to use the App. If you contact us for support, we may collect the information you provide (e.g., your email address or phone number).
  • Data collected automatically: When you use the App, third-party services (like Google AdMob) may automatically collect certain information about your device (e.g., Advertising ID, IP address) to serve personalized ads.
  • Files you open or edit: The App processes your files locally on your device. We do not upload your PDFs, images, or extracted text to our servers.

Permissions & Device Access

To provide its features, the App requests the following permissions:

  • Storage (Read/Write): Required to open PDF files from your device and save your edited or created documents (e.g., "Save As", "Compress PDF").
  • Camera: Required specifically for the Scan to PDF feature. Images captured via the camera are processed locally to generate PDF pages and are not sent to external servers.
  • Internet/Network: Required solely for displaying advertisements (Google AdMob) and loading external links (e.g., Privacy Policy).
Important: Your documents, signatures, and scanned images are processed locally. We do not have access to your files.

3. Why we collect data (purposes)

  • To facilitate PDF editing, creation, and scanning functionalities on your device.
  • To display advertisements (via Google AdMob) which support the maintenance of the App.
  • To respond to user support requests (via WhatsApp or Email).
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

4. Third-party services and advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers, location data, and other network information to provide personalized ads and analytics.

By using the App, you consent to the processing of data by Google in the manner and for the purposes set out in their privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How AdMob uses data

5. Data security

Since the App processes your files locally, your sensitive documents remain on your device. We do not store your files on our servers. For any data transmitted for advertising purposes (by Google), standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) are used.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a general audience productivity tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Your rights and choices

Advertising choices: You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings (e.g., Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization).

Data Access: Since we do not store user accounts or files on our servers, you retain full control and possession of your data on your device.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our features or legal requirements. We will update the "Effective date" at the top of this policy when changes occur.

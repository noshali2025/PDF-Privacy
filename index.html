Privacy Policy

Arriverr PDF Reader (“we”, “us”, or “Arriverr”) operates the https://pdfprivacy.netlify.app website and the Arriverr PDF Reader mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the App.

Effective date: December 29, 2025

1. Summary

Arriverr PDF Reader is a productivity/utility application for viewing, scanning and editing PDF documents. The App includes features such as Text→PDF, PDF→Image (including long images), Scan→PDF, Compress PDF, Lock/Unlock PDF, Split & Merge PDF, HTML→PDF and PDF editing tools. The App displays advertisements and may offer in-app purchases. We do not collect personal information from users unless explicitly provided for support or feedback purposes.

2. Information We Collect

  • Automatically Collected Information: When you use the App, we may collect non-personal device information such as device type, OS version, and app usage statistics to improve performance. This is anonymized and does not identify you personally.
  • PDF Files and Content: The App processes PDF files stored on your device. We do not access, store, or transmit your PDF content to our servers unless you choose to share it (e.g., via email support).
  • Advertisements: We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect anonymized data for personalized advertising, such as device ID and location (if permitted). See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Camera and Storage Access: The App requests permission to access your camera (for scanning) and storage (for file handling). This is used solely for app functionality and not for data collection.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App's features (e.g., PDF editing, compression).
  • To display relevant advertisements via AdMob.
  • To respond to user inquiries or support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With service providers like Google AdMob for ad serving.
  • If required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission is 100% secure. Use the App at your own risk.

6. Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For ads. Review Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Syncfusion Libraries: For PDF processing (local only, no data shared).
  • Google ML Kit: For OCR (local processing, no data sent to servers).

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your jurisdiction, subject to applicable data protection laws.

9. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us. For ad personalization, manage settings in your device or AdMob.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App does not use cookies, but third-party ads may use tracking technologies. Opt out via device settings.

11. Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for app functionality or to comply with legal obligations as applicable.
